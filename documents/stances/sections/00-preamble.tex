\section{Preamble}\label{preamble}

\subsection{Mission}\label{mission}

One of the core mandates of the Canadian Federation of Engineering Students (CFES) is to advocate to relevant stakeholders on behalf of over 80,000 engineering students across Canada in 45 higher education institutions (HEIs). Over the years, the Federation has developed partnerships with organizations such as the Canadian Engineering Accreditation Board (CEAB) and Engineering Deans Canada (EDC) that have empowered the CFES to advocate on behalf of engineering students, influence decision making regarding the engineering curriculum and shape the future of the engineering profession.

\subsection{The CFES Advocacy Portfolio in Brief}\label{the-cfes-advocacy-portfolio-in-brief}

There are two key activities that inform the CFES' advocacy: the national stances and national surveys. The stances are intended to be updated regularly to reflect advances and setbacks in engineering student advocacy and are largely based on data collected from national student surveys which are intended to be released once a year.

The CFES has released three national surveys to date: 2017, 2020 on COVID-19-specific themes and 2023 based around student development and support. The CFES passed its first stances during a General Assembly at the Canadian Engineering Leadership Conference (then known as Congress) 2018, and have since been updated in 2024.

\subsection{The Document of Stances}\label{the-document-of-stances}

The Document of Stances summarizes the CFES' official member-approved stances to outline advocacy efforts in various aspects of the engineering student experience. Both the Document of Stances and the individual full-length stances are available publicly to members with their own advocacy efforts. These are inspired by Engineers Canada's position papers and serve as a valuable resource for members to inform institutional advocacy initiatives.
