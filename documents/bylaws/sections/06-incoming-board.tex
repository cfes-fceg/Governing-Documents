\section{Incoming Board}
\label{incoming-board}

\subsection{Responsibilities}
\label{responsibilities-incoming-board}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  The Incoming Board shall have the following responsibilities:
  \begin{enumerate}
   \item
    Familiarizing themselves with the Constitution, Bylaws, and Policy Manual of the Federation;
   \item
    Assisting the organizational transition process;
   \item
    Attending Board Meetings as non-voting members of the Board;
   \item
    Preparing for the commencement of their term of office.
  \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Transition}
\label{transition-incoming-board}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  Each incumbent Board Member and their incoming counterpart shall collaborate throughout the transition period to ensure that the incoming Board Member is able to fully execute the responsibilities of their portfolio at the commencement of their term of office.
\end{enumerate}
