\section{Chair}
\label{chair}

\subsection{Purpose}
\label{chair-purpose}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item To provide leadership and oversight for the Board and General Meetings.
 \item To facilitate orderly, transparent, and effective governance and decision-making.
 \item To ensure that meetings are properly convened, prepared for, conducted, and documented in accordance with the Constitution and Bylaws.
 \item To support effective communication and participation among Members and the Leadership Team.
 \item To ensure the organization maintains proper corporate governance and fulfills its obligations under the CNCA and applicable law.
 \item To serve as the organization’s primary point of responsibility for incorporation and ongoing corporate compliance matters.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Responsibilities}
\label{chair-responsibilities}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item Chairing meetings of the Board, including:
  \begin{enumerate}
   \item Calling meetings of the Board in accordance with the Constitution.
   \item Distributing meeting minutes to the Board for approval.
   \item Submitting approved minutes to be distributed publicly.
  \end{enumerate}
 \item Chairing General Meetings, including:
  \begin{enumerate}
   \item Ensuring, in conjunction with the relevant Activity Manager, that sufficient time and resources are scheduled to complete all proceedings,
   \item Ensuring that Members are made aware of the meetings as required by the Constitution,
   \item Ensuring that Members are adequately prepared for the meeting by providing them with previous meeting minutes, and any other relevant documents,
   \item Ensuring that a process exists for Members to submit proposals and counterproposals,
   \item Ensuring that a preliminary agenda is circulated as required for each meeting,
  \end{enumerate}
 \item In the absence of the Chair, the responsibilities above are to be completed by the Vice-Chair.
  \begin{enumerate}
   \item The Board shall designate a member of the Leadership Team as Vice-Chair, who is to fulfill the duties of the Chair in their absence.
   \item The President shall not be eligible to fill the position of Vice-Chair.
  \end{enumerate}
 \item Acting as the ex-officio Chief Returning Officer
 \item Dealing with all incorporation matters, including filing the Annual Summary with Corporations Canada each year.
 \item Ensuring continued compliance with the CNCA and applicable law.
\end{enumerate}
