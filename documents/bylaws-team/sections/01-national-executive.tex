\section{National Executive}
\label{national-executive}

\subsection{Overview of National Executive}
\label{national-executive-overview-of-national-executive}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item The National Executive is the elected leadership of the CFES and consists of the following five positions:
  \begin{enumerate}
   \item President
   \item Vice President Academic
   \item Vice President Finance
   \item Vice President External
   \item Vice President Services
   \item Vice President Communications
  \end{enumerate}
 \item The National Executive's general tasks and responsibilities include:
  \begin{enumerate}
   \item Upholding the Constitution, Bylaws, and Policies of the CFES
   \item Organizing and directing the efforts of the CFES
   \item Ensuring the completion of all mandates
   \item Managing Commissioners within their respective portfolios, and assuming responsibilities in the event they are not completed
   \item Making contact with government bodies when necessary
   \item Hiring working groups for their successors as required by April 30th
   \item Creating a transition document by April 30th to be stored in the electronic CFES archive
  \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{President}
\label{national-executive-president}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item The President’s tasks and responsibilities include:
  \begin{enumerate}
   \item Providing leadership and oversight for the National Executive
   \item Ensuring the mandates of each Vice President are completed
   \item Organizing meetings of the National Executive
   \item Ensuring that long term planning for the CFES is undertaken, with annual updates given to the Members
   \item Maintaining communication channels with all stakeholders of the CFES
   \item Ensuring communication with Engineers Canada and attending the Engineers Canada Annual General Meeting and Board Meetings, if feasible
  \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Vice President Academic (VPA)}
\label{national-executive-vice-president-academic-vpa}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item The VPA’s tasks and responsibilities include:
  \begin{enumerate}
   \item Developing an understanding of academic initiatives, integrated work experience, curriculum content, accreditation standards and requirements as well as any changes which could affect engineering education
   \item Determining the academic, moral, intellectual, cultural, social, and economic needs of Canadian engineering students, working towards changes if necessary, and offering national leverage in issues of negotiation between student representation and administration at Members’ institutions and other relevant organizations
   \item Communicating relevant information and issues related to engineering education or that affect the Canadian engineering student population to Members and Engineering Deans Canada
   \item Facilitating the exchange of relevant academic information and advocacy efforts between Members and national educational organizations
   \item Attending Canadian Engineering Accreditation Board and Engineering Deans Canada meetings and providing highlights of the meetings to the Board
   \item Managing advocacy in relation to to the Document of Stances
   \item Directing the Advocacy Working Group, which shall support the VPA portfolio through advocacy tasks including but not limited to research, resource creation, and statement writing on topics related to the CFES's Stances or other concerns from Members.
   \item Providing support to the advocacy efforts of Members where it aligns with the CFES’s efforts, including but not limited to letters of support.
  \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Vice President Finance (VPF)}
\label{national-executive-vice-president-finance-vpf}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item The VPF’s tasks and responsibilities include:
  \begin{enumerate}
   \item Acting as the treasurer of CFES by:
    \begin{enumerate}
     \item Issuing outgoing payments
     \item Depositing incoming funds
     \item Tracking all financial assets
     \item Keeping a complete and accurate record of all previous years’ financial records
     \item Creating and maintaining current and historical financial statements (income statement, balance sheet and cash flow statement)
     \item Ensuring an annual audit of the CFES’ finances is completed
    \end{enumerate}
   \item Ensuring the timely change of Signing Officers on the CFES accounts at the end of the fiscal year.
   \item Preparing a list of membership fee collection information for the current and previous year
   \item Collecting the number of students represented by each Member each year
   \item Submitting a proposed detailed budget for the incoming year to the Board
   \item Documenting the support for expenses and revenues, including receipts, invoices, minutes approving expenditures, and any other necessary documents.
  \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Outgoing Vice-President Finance}
\label{vice-president-finance-vpf-outgoing-vice-president-finance}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item The Outgoing VPF’s tasks and responsibilities include:
  \begin{enumerate}
   \item Working alongside the VPF through the early months of their term
   \item Finalizing the financial statements for the fiscal year of their mandate
   \item Ensuring the completion of the audit for the fiscal year of their mandate.
  \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Vice President External (VPE)}
\label{national-executive-vice-president-external-vpe}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item The VPE’s tasks and responsibilities include:
  \begin{enumerate}
   \item Acting as the primary contact of the CFES to existing and prospective national and international partners
   \item Exploring new projects and cooperation ideas with existing and prospective national and international partners
   \item Ensuring that all partners and developing partners are invited to all relevant events
   \item Promoting international opportunities and the benefits of international cooperation to the Members
   \item Providing regular updates on relevant projects, initiatives, and ongoing work of partners and the outcomes of these partnerships
  \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Vice President Services (VPS)}
\label{national-executive-vice-president-services-vps}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item The VPS’s tasks and responsibilities include:
  \begin{enumerate}
   \item Being chiefly responsible for all CFES Activities and overseeing Activity Managers.
   \item Planning, scheduling, and hosting monthly check-in meetings with the Activity Managers.
   \item Ensuring that the responsibilities outlined in the Activity Agreements are completed.
   \item Organizing transition reports, transition meetings with outgoing and incoming team, and financial closeout of Activities.
   \item Encouraging Members to bid and host Activities
   \item Supporting Members in their bids to host Activities
   \item Collecting feedback from delegates following Activities within a month of the completion of the Activity.
  \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Vice President Communications (VPC)}
\label{national-executive-vice-president-communications-vpc}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item The VPC’s tasks and responsibilities include:
  \begin{enumerate}
   \item Maintaining and upkeeping an archive of original files relating to the logo, banners, branded items, and templates of the CFES.
   \item Maintaining and enforcing the logo and branding guidelines of the CFES
   \item Assisting Activities with the creation of design templates for documents or promotional materials, as necessary
   \item Sourcing quotes for branded items for the CFES or its Activities as necessary
   \item Updating social media profiles for the CFES
   \item Directing the Media \& Marketing Working Group, which shall make graphics, research trends, and provide new ideas on how to best use the CFES socials.
   \item Directing the Bilingualism Working Group, which shall support translation, make plans to improve bilingualism at Activities or online meetings, and help make bilingualism resources for others.
   \item Facilitating engagement between Members throughout the year.
   \item Sending out publications to the Members every other month to keep them up to date with the progress of the CFES Team.
  \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
