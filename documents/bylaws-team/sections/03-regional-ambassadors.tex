\section{Regional Ambassadors}
\label{regional-ambassadors}

\subsection{Purpose}
\label{regional-ambassadors-purpose}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item Regional Ambassadors are nominated by their respective region to represent them on the Board.
 \item The four Regional Ambassadors shall be:
  \begin{enumerate}
   \item West Ambassador
   \item Ontario Ambassador
   \item Quebec Ambassador
   \item Atlantic Ambassador
  \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Regional Agreements}
\label{regional-ambassadors-regional-agreements}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item The CFES shall maintain contracts with the organizations corresponding to the regions of the CFES, known as Regional Agreements.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Responsibilities}
\label{regional-ambassadors-responsibilities}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item Regional Ambassadors’ tasks and responsibilities include:
  \begin{enumerate}
   \item Maintaining open lines of communication between CFES and Members in their region, and bringing their region’s perspective forward in Board meetings.
   \item Assisting in the review Regional Agreements annually
   \item Facilitating the use of proxy votes in General Meetings as necessary.
   \item Encouraging the members of their region to participate in and/or organize CFES events
   \item Encouraging students to run for CFES positions.
   \item Assisting the VPF in the collection of membership fees.
   \item Conducting CFES introduction and update sessions for Members in their region.
   \item Ensuring the CFES is represented at major regional activities, such as AGMs, regional competitions and regional meetings.
   \item Holding regional meetings at CELC and any other relevant Activities.
   \item Meeting with the Members of their region before all Activities to inform the Members of expectations for the Activity.
   \item Meet with their constituencies after all General Meetings to review the outcomes of the General Meetings.
   \item Creating a transition document by April 30th to be stored in the electronic CFES archive.
  \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
