\section{Membership}
\label{membership}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item A Member is a society that:
  \begin{enumerate}
   \item represents undergraduate engineering students at a Canadian higher education institution has an engineering program accredited, or in the process of accreditation, by the Canadian Engineering Accreditation Board
   \item represents the students' concerns to their faculty and higher education institution administration
   \item has been admitted to membership in the CFES in accordance with this Constitution.
  \end{enumerate}
 \item Multiple societies may apply to co-represent the undergraduate engineering students at their institution where there is no single society fitting the description.
  \begin{enumerate}
   \item The voting representative must be jointly confirmed by the President of each society at each General Meeting.
  \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Application for Membership}
\label{membership-application-for-membership}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item To become a Member, an organization must submit an application, the content of which shall be at the discretion of the Board.
 \item The Board shall then pass a Resolution to accept or reject the organization's admission, and this no later than the General Meeting following the organisation's submission of the application.
 \item The following General Meeting shall ratify the organisation’s membership by Resolution.
\end{enumerate}
