\section{Amendments}
\label{amendments}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item Subject to the Articles, the Board may, by Resolution, make, amend or repeal any provisions of this Constitution that regulate the activities or affairs of the CFES, except in respect of matters referred to in subsection 197(1) of the Act.
 \item Any such amendment or repeal shall be effective from the date of the Board Resolution until the next General Meeting where it may be confirmed, rejected or amended by the Members by Resolution.
  \begin{enumerate}
   \item If the amendment or repeal is confirmed or confirmed as amended by the Members, it remains effective in the form in which it was confirmed.
   \item The amendment or repeal ceases to have effect if it is not submitted to the Members at the next General Meeting or if it is rejected by the Members at the General Meeting.
   \item If an amendment or a repeal ceases to have effect, a subsequent Board resolution that has substantially the same purpose or effect is not effective until it is confirmed, or confirmed as amended, by the Members at a General Meeting.
   \item This does not apply to any amendment that requires a Special Resolution of the Members under subsection 197(1) (fundamental change) of the Act because such amendments or repeals are only effective when confirmed by the Members.
  \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
