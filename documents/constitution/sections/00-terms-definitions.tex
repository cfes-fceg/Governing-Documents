 Term                   & Definition                                                                                                                                                                                    \\
 \hline
 CFES                   & Abbreviation for the Canadian Federation of Engineering Students                                                                                                                              \\
 Articles               & The original or restated articles of incorporation or articles of amendment, amalgamation, continuance, reorganization, arrangement or revival of CFES.                                       \\
 Resolution             & A motion presented as a proposal and approved by majority vote, or the applicable voting threshold if otherwise specified                                                                     \\
 Special Resolution     & A Resolution approved by a two-thirds (2/3) majority vote                                                                                                                                     \\
 Member, Member Society & A recognized engineering student society representing students at a Canadian post-secondary institution that has been admitted to membership in the CFES in accordance with this Constitution \\
 National Executive     & The President and the Vice-Presidents                                                                                                                                                         \\
 Leadership Team        & The Directors and National Executive                                                                                                                                                          \\
 CFES Team              & The Leadership Team, Commissioners, and Activity Managers                                                                                                                                     \\
 Activity               & A conference, competition, or other event organized by the CFES for its Members                                                                                                               \\
 Host Society           & The Member Society responsible for an Activity of the CFES                                                                                                                                    \\
 Activity Manager(s)    & The person chiefly responsible for the organization of an Activity on behalf of a Host Society                                                                                                \\
 General Meeting        & A meeting of the Members                                                                                                                                                                      \\
 CELC                   & The Canadian Engineering Leadership Conference                                                                                                                                                \\
 CSE                    & The Conference on Sustainability in Engineering.                                                                                                                                              \\
 CEC                    & The Canadian Engineering Competition                                                                                                                                                          \\
 CESS                   & The Canadian Engineering Spring Summit, where the Annual General Meeting is held                                                                                                              \\
 SDES                   & The Summit on the Development of Engineering Societies                                                                                                                                        \\
 CDE                    & The Conference on Diversity in Engineering                                                                                                                                                    \\
\end{longtblr}
