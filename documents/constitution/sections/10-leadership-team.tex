\section{Leadership Team}
\label{leadership-team}

\subsection{Remuneration}
\label{leadership-team-remuneration}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item The Leadership Team shall serve without remuneration and no member of the Leadership Team shall directly or indirectly receive any profit from holding the position, provided that reasonable expenses incurred in the exercise of the member’s duties are reimbursed.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Accountability}
\label{leadership-team-accountability}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item The Leadership Team shall remain accountable to the Members.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Dismissal}
\label{leadership-team-dismissal}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item A member of the Leadership Team may be dismissed by a Resolution of the General Meeting
 \item A member of the National Executive may also be dismissed by a Special Resolution of the Board.
  \begin{enumerate}
   \item The Board does not have the authority to dismiss the President.
  \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Vacancy}
\label{leadership-team-vacancy}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item Provided a vacancy occurs for any Leadership Team position, this vacancy shall be communicated to the Members within five (5) days.
 \item A vacancy which occurs due to the removal of a Director by Resolution of a General Meeting may be filled at that General Meeting by Resolution. If the Director vacancy is not filled at that General Meeting, then the Board may fill the vacant Director position by appointment.
 \item The Board may, by appointment, fill the vacancy of any Leadership Team position (with the exception of a vacancy to a Director position resulting from the removal of a Director at a General Meeting, which must be filled in in the manner described in Section 10.4.2 above).
  \begin{enumerate}
   \item Vacancies in the position of Regional Ambassador may be filled in consultation with the affected region.
   \item The Board may appoint one or more additional Directors who shall hold office for a term expiring no later than the close of the next Annual General Meeting, but the total number of Directors so appointed may not exceed one third of the number of Directors elected at the previous Annual General Meeting of the members.
  \end{enumerate}
 \item In the event of a vacancy of a Vice Presidential position, the President shall assume the portfolio’s responsibilities.
 \item In the event of a vacancy of a Director’s position, the Board shall assume the portfolio’s responsibilities.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Concurrent Positions}
\label{leadership-team-concurrent-positions}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item No person shall hold more than one position on the Leadership Team at the same time.
\end{enumerate}
