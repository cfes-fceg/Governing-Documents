\section{Invalidation}
\label{invalidation}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item The invalidity or unenforceability of any provision of this Constitution shall not affect the validity or enforceability of the remaining provisions of this Constitution. If any of the provisions contained in the Constitution are inconsistent with those contained in the Articles or the Act, the provisions contained in the Articles or the Act, as the case may be, shall prevail.
 \item Titles inserted in this Constitution and in all other documents of the CFES hereafter passed are included only to facilitate reading and should not affect interpretation.
 \item In this Constitution and in all other documents of the CFES hereafter passed, unless the context otherwise requires, words importing the singular number shall include the plural number, and vice versa, and references to persons shall include firms and corporations.
 \item In the case of a discrepancy between the English and French versions of this constitution, the English version takes precedence.
\end{enumerate}
