\section{National Councillors}
\label{national-councillors}

\subsection{Purpose}
\label{national-councillors-purpose}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item The National Councillors shall provide the Board with continuity and institutional knowledge, and shall advise on any matter brought before them.
 \item The National Councillors shall be responsible for assisting the organizational transition process.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Election}
\label{national-councillors-election}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item The two (2) National Councillors shall be elected at the Annual General Meeting.
 \item There is no specific eligibility condition for the candidate for a National Councillor position.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Term}
\label{national-councillors-term}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item The term of office for the National Councillors shall commence on the date of their appointment and expire at the close of the next year’s Annual General Meeting.
\end{enumerate}
