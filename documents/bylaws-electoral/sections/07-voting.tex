\section{Voting}
\label{voting}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item Voting shall occur by means of secret ballot using a transferable-vote system.
  \begin{enumerate}
   \item For elections to a single position, the Instant-Runoff Voting (IRV) method shall be used.
    \begin{enumerate}
     \item In the event that no candidate obtains more than 50\% of the vote following this procedure, no candidate shall be declared elected or nominated, and a new election may be held.
    \end{enumerate}
   \item Where two or more positions are to be filled, the positions shall be filled sequentially using IRV. Following the election or nomination of each candidate, that candidate shall be removed from the remaining ballots, and the process shall be repeated until all positions have been filled.
    \begin{enumerate}
     \item A candidate must obtain more than 50\% of the votes to be declared elected or nominated, as the case may be.
    \end{enumerate}
   \item The National Executive shall submit a ranked list of candidates to the CRO to only be used in the event of a tie.
  \end{enumerate}
 \item The procedures for administering and counting votes shall be established by the CRO in accordance with these Bylaws.
 \item Votes shall be counted by the CRO.
  \begin{enumerate}
   \item The General Meeting may appoint, by Resolution, a scrutineer, who shall supervise the counting.
  \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
