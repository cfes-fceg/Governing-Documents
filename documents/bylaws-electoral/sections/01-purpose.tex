\section{Purpose}
\label{purpose}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item These Electoral Bylaws provide for:
  \begin{enumerate}
   \item the selection by the Members of a preferred slate of Leadership Team nominees at a General Meeting held before the Annual General Meeting;
   \item the presentation of that slate to the Members at the Annual General Meeting for formal election;
   \item the collective election of the slate through a single ordinary resolution where the election is uncontested;
   \item the continued right of Members to nominate additional eligible candidates from the floor at the Annual General Meeting; and
   \item a fair election process where additional nominations result in more candidates than available Leadership Team positions.
   \item The selection of the Slate of Nominees at the Nomination Meeting is preliminary only. No individual becomes a member of the Leadership Team by reason of being selected for the Slate of Nominees. Members of the Leadership Team are formally elected by the Members at the Annual General Meeting.
  \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
