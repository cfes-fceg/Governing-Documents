\section{Chief Returning Officer}
\label{chief-returning-officer}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item The CRO shall be responsible for conducting any elections and nominations of the CFES.
 \item The Chair shall serve as the CRO ex officio.
  \begin{enumerate}
   \item The Board may, by Special Resolution, appoint another individual to serve as CRO for any election.
   \item In the case of a conflict of interest, such appointment shall be mandatory.
  \end{enumerate}
 \item The CRO shall be responsible for the resolution of any conflicts, ambiguities, and interpretations of the electoral and nomination procedures.
 \item All decisions of the CRO shall be final.
\end{enumerate}
