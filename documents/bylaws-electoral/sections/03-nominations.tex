\section{Nominations}
\label{nominations}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item Nominations of candidates for Leadership Team positions shall occur by vote at a General Meeting in advance of the Annual General Meeting each year. The Members shall vote on a slate of candidates to be put forward for election at the Annual General Meeting.
 \item Candidates for any elected position shall first be nominated by ten (10) members of Member Societies.
  \begin{enumerate}
   \item Nomination forms shall be distributed to prospective candidates by the CRO.
  \end{enumerate}
 \item No candidate shall be nominated for multiple positions in the same election.
 \item Eligible candidates must:
  \begin{enumerate}
   \item be at least 18 years of age;
   \item not have been declared incapable under the laws of a Canadian province or territory, or by a court in a jurisdiction outside of Canada;
   \item be an individual (a corporation cannot be a Director or member of the Leadership Team);
   \item not be in bankrupt status; and
   \item consent in writing to serve as a Director and/or member of the Leadership Team, if elected.
  \end{enumerate}
 \item Voting to determine the slate of candidates shall occur in the manner described in Section 8 of these Electoral Bylaws.
 \item The selected slate of candidates shall be put forth for election at the Annual General Meeting.
 \item If a nominated candidate withdraws, becomes ineligible or is unable to stand for election before the Annual General Meeting:
  \begin{enumerate}
   \item that candidate’s position on the Slate of Nominees shall become vacant;
   \item the Board shall not substitute another candidate on its own authority;
   \item the Board may call a further Nomination Meeting if time permits; and
   \item if no further Nomination Meeting is held, the position may be filled through a nomination at the Annual General Meeting.
  \end{enumerate}
 \item Upon being nominated, each nominee must fulfill prerequisite Leadership Team nominee transition training activities as prescribed by the Leadership Team prior to the Annual General Meeting. Any nominee who fails to complete such transition training activities shall be deemed to have withdrawn their candidacy and shall be replaced in the manner described in Section 4.7 of these Electoral Bylaws.
 \item For clarity, a candidate chosen as part of the Slate of Nominees shall not be considered to have been elected, appointed, or approved as a Leadership Team until the candidate has been formally elected at the Annual General Meeting.
 \item Nothing in these Electoral Bylaws shall preclude a nomination made from the floor at the Annual General Meeting in accordance with section 163(5) of the Canada Not-For-Profit Corporations Act. A candidate nominated in such manner shall be included alongside the Slate of Nominees for election at the Annual General Meeting.
\end{enumerate}
