\section{Invalidation}
\label{invalidation}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item The invalidity or unenforceability of any provision of these Bylaws shall not affect the validity or enforceability of the remaining provisions of these Bylaws. If any of the provisions contained in the Bylaws are inconsistent with those contained in the Constitution, the Articles or the Canada Not-For-Profit Corporations Act, the provisions contained in the Constitution, the Articles or the Act, as the case may be, shall prevail.
 \item Titles inserted in these Bylaws are included only to facilitate reading and should not affect their interpretation.
 \item In these Bylaws and in all other documents of the CFES hereafter passed, unless the context otherwise requires, words importing the singular number shall include the plural number, and vice versa, and references to persons shall include firms and corporations.
 \item In the case of a discrepancy between the English and French versions of these Bylaws, the English version takes precedence.
\end{enumerate}
