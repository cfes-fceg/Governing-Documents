\section{Campaigning}
\label{campaigning}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item All candidates shall be allowed to produce a document outlining their platforms which shall be distributed to the Members.
  \begin{enumerate}
   \item The format, length, and timeline for submitting this document shall be determined by the CRO and announced prior to the nomination period.
   \item The document shall be distributed in both official languages.
  \end{enumerate}
 \item Any other regulations regarding campaigning shall be at the discretion of the CRO.
\end{enumerate}
