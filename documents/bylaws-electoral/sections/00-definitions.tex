 Term                   & Definition                                                                                                                                                                                    \\
 \hline
 CFES                   & Abbreviation for the Canadian Federation of Engineering Students                                                                                                                              \\
 Articles               & The original or restated articles of incorporation or articles of amendment, amalgamation, continuance, reorganization, arrangement or revival of CFES.                                       \\
 Resolution             & A motion presented as a proposal and approved by majority vote, or the applicable voting threshold if otherwise specified                                                                     \\
 Special Resolution     & A Resolution approved by a two-thirds (2/3) majority vote                                                                                                                                     \\
 Member, Member Society & A recognized engineering student society representing students at a Canadian post-secondary institution that has been admitted to membership in the CFES in accordance with this Constitution \\
 General Meeting        & A meeting of the Members                                                                                                                                                                      \\
 CRO                    & The Chief Returning Officer, individual who is responsible for and the ultimate authority on election matters                                                                                 \\
 Nomination Meeting     & The General Meeting held before the Annual General Meeting for the purpose of selecting the slate of nominee candidates to fill upcoming vacancies.                                           \\
 Slate of Nominees      & The slate of Leadership Team candidates selected by the Members at the Nomination Meeting for presentation at the Annual General Meeting.                                                     \\
 National Executive     & The President and Vice-Presidents                                                                                                                                                             \\
 Leadership Team        & The Directors and National Executive                                                                                                                                                          \\
\end{longtblr}
