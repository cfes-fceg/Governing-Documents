\section{Election at the Annual General Meeting}
\label{election-at-the-annual-general-meeting}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item Where the Slate of Nominees is uncontested and no additional nominations have been made that would result in the number of candidates exceeding the number of available Leadership Team positions, the Slate of Nominees shall be presented together for election by Resolution of the General Meeting. If the Slate of Nominees is approved by a majority of the votes cast, then each candidate on the Slate of Nominees shall be deemed elected.
 \item If the Slate of Nominees is not approved by Resolution of the General Meeting, then the name of each nominee named on the Slate of Nominees shall remain eligible to stand as a candidate for election unless they withdraw. The CRO shall reopen nominations from the floor, and the election shall proceed in the manner described in Section 8 below. Nominated candidates must fulfill all eligibility criteria contained in the Constitution and these Electoral Bylaws.
 \item Where additional candidates have been nominated or the total number of candidates on the Slate of Nominees exceeds the number of available positions, each candidate shall be voted upon in the manner described in Section 8 below.
\end{enumerate}
