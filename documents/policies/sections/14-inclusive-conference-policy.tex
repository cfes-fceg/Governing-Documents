\section{Inclusive Conference Guidelines}
\label{inclusive-conference-guidelines}

\subsection{Purpose}
\label{purpose-3}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  To best serve our members, it is essential that the Federation's conferences and activities be safe spaces that encourage discussion and learning amongst its members.
 \item
  To this end, it shall be the policy of the Federation to facilitate the following:

  \begin{enumerate}
   \item
    A Conference Code of Conduct;
   \item
    An Incident Reporting System, with a Response Committee at each Activity;
   \item
    Inclusivity Orientation at CELC every year
   \item
    Provide Inclusive Conference Planning information to Activity Managers upon ratification
   \item
    Inclusion in pre-delegate packages of information on accessibility features available at the main venue(s) of any Activity.
   \item
    A Conference Expectations Debrief for all delegates before the conference
  \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Conference Code of Conduct}
\label{conference-code-of-conduct}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  The CFES Conference Code of Conduct shall be distributed at least one month before each Activity, and shall be as follows:
\end{enumerate}

\emph{The Canadian Federation of Engineering Students aims to promote
 inclusive and respectful environments where all students feel safe,
 welcome, and valued. To accomplish this, the CFES expects students to
 behave in an inclusive and respectful manner befitting a future
 engineer.}

\emph{By attending a CFES conference, you commit to:}

\begin{itemize}
 \item
  \emph{Value other people's experiences;}
 \item
  \emph{Embody a spirit of respect and fellowship;}
 \item
  \emph{Maintain respectful communication regardless of disagreements;}
 \item
  \emph{Have an open mind for new ideas and information;}
 \item
  \emph{Refrain from behaving in a manner that degrades, insults, or
   otherwise negatively affects others;}
 \item
  \emph{Support your peers; and}
 \item
  \emph{Grow and develop professionally, academically, and socially.}
 \item
  \emph{Respect other people's privacy and adhere to a respectful
   rooming etiquette.}
\end{itemize}

\emph{The CFES is based on the combined effort, dedication, and
 commitment of its students. Abiding by this Code of Conduct actively
 contributes to building a better CFES and promotes positive CFES

\subsection{Accessibility Information}
\label{accessibility-information}

\subsubsection{Pre-Delegate Package Content}
\label{pre-delegate-package-content}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  To ensure that potential delegates are aware in advance of any obstacles to their participation which may be present at a conference, pre-delegate packages for all activities will include information on the following:

  \begin{enumerate}
   \item
    The presence of accessible accommodations and obstacles for those with limited mobility
   \item
    Any restrictions on service animals which may exist at the Activity's main venue(s)
   \item
    The availability or lack of availability of food meeting the requirements of common dietary restrictions, including vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, lactose-free, halal, and kosher options.
   \item
    The availability or lack of availability of translation resources such as define in Section 13.4.
   \item
    A list of the common words and official abbreviations with their meaning at conferences.
   \item
    Other information as determined by the relevant Activity Manager or the VP Services.
  \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Conference Accountability}
\label{conference-accountability}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  The VPS and OC of the conference must meet at least three times before the conference to ensure that Section 13.3 is fulfilled.

  \begin{enumerate}
   \item
    The first meeting is to happen at least 6 months before the conference.
   \item
    The second meeting is to happen at least 1 month before the pre-delegate package is sent.
   \item
    The third meeting is to happen at least 3 weeks before the conference start.
  \end{enumerate}

 \item
  The VPS and OC of the respective conference must meet after the conference to discuss whether the accessibility expectations were fulfilled.
 \item
  The Board assigns a warning status to the host of the investigated conference if there is a lack of professionalism from the host and it is host's first time.
 \item
  The warning status is to be removed if the same host runs a successful conference.
 \item
  When the warned host plans another conference with the warning status is still being held and the conference still has a problem, the Board is to place the member school in bad standing.
 \item
  In case the report displayed a lack of planning from the OC of the respective conference, the membership can mandate the Board to perform an investigation.
 \item
  The Board is to share the findings and results of the investigation with the member schools within a month after the investigated member school is being notified of the results.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Translation Service Guidelines}
\label{translation-service-guidelines}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  The following items are the possible resources that can be available at conferences:

  \begin{enumerate}
   \item
    Bilingual PowerPoint, signs and documents made for the conference
   \item
    Bilingual presenter
   \item
    Simultaneous translation : subtitles, bilingual volunteers/delegate with unilingual presenter
   \item
    Translated script
   \item
    Translated session minutes
   \item
    Cards with common words distributed to delegates
   \item
    Providing resources or immersive training to key members/CFES Team : teaching basic vocabulary, relevant words and key terms
   \item
    Easily identifiable bilingualism delegate
  \end{enumerate}

\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Rooming Lists}
\label{rooming-lists}

\subsubsection{Overview}
\label{rooming-lists-overview}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  The rooming list is an important part of the delegate experience at CFES Activities. Delegates should feel safe and comfortable in their accommodations throughout the conference.
 \item
  Activity Managers are responsible for collecting rooming preferences from all delegates and producing a rooming list that best accommodates those preferences.
 \item
  The rooming list must be approved by the Board before it is submitted to the hotel or accommodation provider.
 \item
  Delegates of different genders shall never be assigned to share a bed.
 \item
  If delegates of different genders specifically request to share a bed, they must have their own room.

  \begin{enumerate}
   \item
    This is at the discretion of the Activity Manager and may result in a higher delegate fee for those individuals.
  \end{enumerate}
 \item
  When producing the rooming list, Activity Managers shall take into account delegate preferences in the following order of priority, at minimum:
  \begin{enumerate}
   \item
    Gender composition of the room;
   \item
    Comfort with alcohol and cannabis consumption in the room; and
   \item
    Sleep schedule, preferred sleep times, and noise level preference.
  \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Standardized Rooming Preference Questions}
\label{standardized-rooming-questions}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  All Activities shall collect rooming preferences from delegates using the standardized questions below. Activities may add additional questions at their discretion.
 \item
  The following note shall accompany question 1: delegates will always share a bed with someone of the same gender; this question pertains to whether the other bed in a double-bed room may be occupied by a delegate of a different gender.
 \item
  The standardized questions are as follows:

  \begin{enumerate}
   \item
    Who are you comfortable sharing a room with?
    \begin{itemize}
     \item Delegates of the same gender as me
     \item Delegates of any gender
    \end{itemize}

   \item
    Would you prefer to room with someone from your school, if possible?
    \begin{itemize}
     \item Yes
     \item No
     \item No Preference
    \end{itemize}

   \item
    Are you okay with having alcohol in your room?
    \begin{itemize}
     \item Yes
     \item No
    \end{itemize}

   \item
    Are you okay with having cannabis in your room?
    \begin{itemize}
     \item Yes
     \item No
    \end{itemize}

   \item
    When do you prefer to go to sleep?
    \begin{itemize}
     \item Early (before midnight)
     \item Late (after midnight)
    \end{itemize}

   \item
    Would you prefer that your roommates go to sleep at roughly the same time as you?
    \begin{itemize}
     \item Yes
     \item No Preference
    \end{itemize}

   \item
    What noise level would you prefer in your room?
    \begin{itemize}
     \item Quiet
     \item Moderate
     \item Loud
     \item No Preference
    \end{itemize}

   \item
    Are you comfortable with other delegates hanging out in your room?
    \begin{itemize}
     \item Yes
     \item Yes, if they are people I know
     \item No
     \item No Preference
    \end{itemize}

   \item
    Are there any specific people you would like to room with? \emph{(open text)}

   \item
    Do you have any other rooming preferences or requests? \emph{(open text)}

  \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Additional Questions for the Canadian Engineering Competition}
\label{cec-rooming-questions}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  In addition to the standardized questions above, the Canadian Engineering Competition (CEC) shall use the following modified and additional questions to account for team composition:

  \begin{enumerate}
   \item
    Who are you comfortable sharing a room with? \emph{(replaces standard question 1 — add the following option)}
    \begin{itemize}
     \item Delegates of any gender (if they are my teammates)
    \end{itemize}

   \item
    Would you like to share a room with your teammates, if possible? \emph{(additional question)}
    \begin{itemize}
     \item Yes
     \item No
     \item No Preference
    \end{itemize}

  \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}
