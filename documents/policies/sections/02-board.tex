\section{Board}
\label{board}

\subsection{Tasks and Responsibilities}
\label{tasks-and-responsibilities}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  The Board' tasks and responsibilities include, but are not limited to:

  \begin{enumerate}
   \item
    Oversight of CFES finances and administration
   \item
    Establishing an agenda in advance of all meetings
   \item
    Determining electoral procedures as needed
   \item
    Allocating, at Spring Meeting, the funds from the travel fund to each major meeting of the CFES during the fiscal year. Such meetings include:

    \begin{enumerate}
     \item
      Summer Meeting
     \item
      Summit on Development of Engineering Societies
     \item
      Fall Meeting
     \item
      CELC
     \item
      Spring Meeting
    \end{enumerate}

   \item
    Tracking progress of the CFES Team

   \item
    Hearing any appeals from the Incident Response Committee
  \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Board Member Conduct}
\label{board-member-conduct}

\subsubsection{Conduct}
\label{conduct}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  Board Members shall act honestly and in good faith in the best interests of the Federation as a whole, and exercise the care, diligence and skill of a reasonably prudent person in carrying out their duties.
 \item
  To ensure Board Members act in this way, the Board is to approve, no later than June 1st of each year, a Code of Conduct which will, at minimum, cover the following topics:

  \begin{enumerate}
   \item
    Conflict of Interest, in accordance with the statements below
   \item
    Culture
   \item
    Responsibilities
   \item
    Confidentiality
  \end{enumerate}

 \item
  Board Members must sign the Code of Conduct approved by the Board no later than June 15th of the year in which they hold office, and the signed copy is to be stored in the organization's records.
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Conflict of Interest for Board Members}
\label{conflict-of-interest-for-board-members}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  No Board Member may, in their role as a Board Member, participate in any decision, exercise an official power or perform an official duty or function in which they may have either a conflict of interest or an apparent conflict of interest.
 \item
  Such circumstances may include, but are not limited to, the following:

  \begin{enumerate}
   \item
    Matters from which a Board Member stands to benefit personally
   \item
    Matters involving a member society of the Federation of which a Board Member is themselves a member
  \end{enumerate}

 \item
  Directors are expected to declare any situation in which they have a conflict of interest, whether real or perceived.
 \item
  In the event a Board Member believes another Board Member has a conflict of interest, the matter shall be brought to the Board Chair for a decision.
 \item
  The Board may overturn the decision of the Board Chair by majority vote.
 \item
  In the event of a conflict of interest for the Board Chair, the Board Chair shall relinquish the chair to the Vice-Chair of the Board until the matter is resolved.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Closed Sessions}
\label{closed-sessions}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  The Board may, from time-to-time, meet in closed session to discuss matters of a confidential nature.
 \item
  Persons eligible to participate in a closed session are the Board, as well as any other individuals included by a resolution requiring a two-thirds (2/3) majority.
 \item
  Matters eligible for discussion in closed session are limited to:

  \begin{enumerate}
   \item
    Contractual negotiations
   \item
    Personnel concerns
   \item
    Matters which pose a reputational risk to the Federation
  \end{enumerate}

 \item
  To move to a closed session, the Board must pass a resolution to do so by a two-thirds (2/3) majority.
 \item
  No resolutions may be passed once the Board has entered a closed session, other than motions to remain in the closed session, which also require a two-thirds (2/3) majority.
 \item
  Persons privy to closed session discussions are required to keep such matters confidential.
 \item
  In addition to those present, only members of the Board not present at a given closed session are entitled to be informed of the content of discussion after the fact.
 \item
  Such information shall only be provided by the Board Chair to the member or members unable to attend.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Board Meeting Agenda and Updates}
\label{board-meeting-agenda-and-updates}

\subsubsection{Agenda Outline}
\label{agenda-outline}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  The business sections of the agenda are, in order:

  \begin{enumerate}
   \item
    Activity Updates
   \item
    Board Updates
   \item
    Financial Updates
   \item
    Previous Business
   \item
    New Business
   \item
    Discussion Topics
   \item
    Notice of Future Motions and Other Business
  \end{enumerate}

\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Agenda Distribution}
\label{agenda-distribution}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  The Board Meeting Agenda shall be shared with the Board and the Activity Managers no later than seven (7) days prior to the meeting.
 \item
  The agenda shall be finalized no later than seventy-two (72) hours prior to the meeting.
 \item
  A copy of the agenda, including all non-confidential motions, may be shared with the Membership after the agenda has been finalized.
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Updates}
\label{board-meeting-updates}

\paragraph{Activity Updates}
\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  Activity Managers shall ensure their updates are added to the agenda no later than seventy-two (72) hours prior to the meeting.
 \item
  Updates shall include all activity within the Activity's portfolio since the last time updates were provided.
 \item
  Updates between the time they were added to the agenda and the meeting may be provided live at the meeting and recorded in the minutes, or added to the following Board Meeting's agenda updates.
 \item
  Activity Managers are expected to track their activities throughout their term so that preparing updates is not an arduous task.
 \item
  There is no reasonable excuse for updates to be absent from the Board Agenda before the agenda is finalized. In the event of other commitments, illness, vacation, or other circumstances, updates for all work completed up to that point in time shall still be added to the agenda or submitted to the VP Services for inclusion in the agenda.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Board Updates}
\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  Board Members shall ensure their updates are added to the agenda no later than seventy-two (72) hours prior to the meeting.
 \item
  Updates shall include all activity within that position's portfolio since the last time updates were provided.
 \item
  Updates between the time they were added to the agenda and the meeting may be provided live at the meeting and recorded in the minutes, or added to the following Board Meeting's agenda updates.
 \item
  Board Members are expected to track their activities throughout their term so that preparing updates is not an arduous task.
 \item
  There is no reasonable excuse for updates to be absent from the Board Agenda before the agenda is finalized. In the event of other commitments, illness, vacation, or other circumstances, updates for all work completed up to that point in time shall still be added to the agenda or submitted to the Board Chair for inclusion in the agenda.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Financial Updates}
\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  Financial updates shall be included in the Board Meeting Agenda in accordance with the \nameref{financial-deliverables} policy.
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Motions}
\label{board-meeting-motions}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  All motions shall be added to the agenda no later than seventy-two (72) hours prior to the meeting.
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Attached Documentation}
\label{attached-documentation}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  All documentation attached to the agenda shall be in PDF format, or a spreadsheet owned by the Board Chair that cannot be edited by other parties, so that meeting materials cannot be changed after the agenda has been finalized.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Written Resolution}
\label{written-resolution}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  In addition to the provisions surrounding written resolutions contained in the Constitution, Board Members shall return to the Board Chair their vote on any written resolution within three (3) business days.
 \item
  Written resolutions may be carried out by electronic means that the Board Chair determines to be secure.
 \item
  The results of a written resolution must be announced by the Board Chair at the next meeting.
\end{enumerate}
