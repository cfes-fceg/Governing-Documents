\section{Digital Platforms \& Services}
\label{digital-platforms-services}

\subsection{Overview}
\label{overview-5}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  The National Executive may employ the use of digital platforms that support the operations of the Federation.
 \item
  All digital platforms and services that require a monthly or annual subscription must be approved by the Board.

  \begin{enumerate}
   \item
    Activity Managers are exempt of this requirement for platforms acquired for their respective Activity
  \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Google Workspace}
\label{google-workspace}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  The Federation utilizes Google Workspace as the central production and collaboration tool, including meetings, e-mail, and file sharing.
 \item
  The following positions should be granted Super Administrator privileges:

  \begin{enumerate}
   \item
    President
   \item
    Vice President Communications
   \item
    Vice President Finance \& Administration
   \item
    Information Technology Commissioner
  \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Flickr}
\label{flickr}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  The Federation utilizes Flickr as the central image hosting platform for CFES events and activities.
 \item
  The Vice President Communications is responsible for the upkeep and management of this platform.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Squarespace}
\label{squarespace}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  The Federation utilizes Squarespace as its website hosting and content management platform.
 \item
  The Vice President Communications shall be responsible for the upkeep and management of this platform.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Activity Email Accounts}
\label{activity-email-accounts}

\subsubsection{Overview}
\label{activity-email-overview}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  The CFES will provision Google Workspace accounts for members of an Activity's Organizing Committee (OC).
 \item
  Each OC member shall be provided with an individual email account, so that each member requires access to only a single account for all Federation-related work.
 \item
  Shared login to a single account by multiple individuals is prohibited, as it presents an information technology security risk and makes document attribution unclear.
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Individual Email Accounts}
\label{individual-email-accounts}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  OC members may be provided with a personal email account in the format:
  \begin{enumerate}
   \item
    \texttt{firstname@activity\{year\}.cfes.ca} (e.g.\ \texttt{alice@conf20XX.cfes.ca}); or
   \item
    \texttt{firstname.initial@activity\{year\}.cfes.ca} or \texttt{firstname.lastname@activity\{year\}.cfes.ca}, in the event that two or more OC members share the same first name.
  \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Role-Based Email Accounts}
\label{role-based-email-accounts}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  Role-based email accounts (e.g.\ \texttt{finance@activity\{year\}.cfes.ca}) shall exist as delegated inboxes only. Direct login to role-based accounts will not be provided; instead, access is granted by delegating the inbox to the relevant OC members\textquotesingle{} individual accounts.

  \begin{enumerate}
   \item
    This allows multiple individuals who share a role to manage a single inbox while retaining separate individual accounts.
   \item
    This also allows an individual to manage multiple role inboxes through their single individual account.
  \end{enumerate}

 \item
  Role-based accounts may not be used as Google Drive accounts or for any Google Workspace storage beyond email.
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Activity Email Domain Options}
\label{activity-email-domain-options}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  Activities may provision email accounts under one of the following domain structures:

  \begin{enumerate}
   \item
    \textbf{Year-scoped accounts} (e.g.\ \texttt{@activity\{year\}.cfes.ca}): Accounts are specific to a given Activity year. The Activity may gain access to these accounts at any time.
   \item
    \textbf{Activity-scoped accounts} (e.g.\ \texttt{@activity.cfes.ca}): Accounts are tied to the Activity name across years. Access to activity-scoped accounts is subject to the following conditions:
    \begin{enumerate}
     \item
      The Activity must wait until the prior year's Activity has occurred before gaining access, regardless of whether the prior year's Activity used activity-scoped accounts.
     \item
      If the prior year's Activity used activity-scoped accounts, the incoming OC must wait until the sooner of: the accounts being ready for transition, or sixty (60) days after the prior Activity occurred.
    \end{enumerate}
  \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Activity Document Organization}
\label{activity-document-organization}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  All files created in the course of planning an Activity shall be stored in a Shared Drive within the Federation's Google Workspace designated for that Activity.
 \item
  The Vice President Services shall be granted Manager access to the Activity's Shared Drive.
 \item
  Documents shall not be stored in personal Google Drive accounts, external cloud storage services, or any storage outside the Federation's Google Workspace.
 \item
  Note: Google Workspace Shared Drives do not support Forms that require file uploads. A shortcut to a folder containing such forms shall be included in the Shared Drive in lieu of storing the forms directly.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Discord}
\label{discord}

\subsubsection{Public Discord Server}
\label{public-discord-server}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  The Federation shall maintain a public Discord server open to students, engineering society presidents, and VP Externals across the country.
 \item
  The Vice President Communications shall own the public Discord server using an account tied to the \texttt{vpc@cfes.ca} email address.
 \item
  The IT Commissioner may be granted administrator access to support the upkeep and technological management of the server.
 \item
  The public Discord server may be used to:

  \begin{enumerate}
   \item
    Facilitate by-elections;
   \item
    Solicit feedback from student leaders;
   \item
    Provide a space for student leaders across the country to communicate; and
   \item
    Host office hours by the National Executive.
  \end{enumerate}

\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Internal Discord Server}
\label{internal-discord-server}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  The Federation shall maintain an internal Discord server for use by the CFES Team.
 \item
  The President shall own the internal Discord server using an account tied to the \texttt{president@cfes.ca} email address.
 \item
  The internal Discord server may be used for discussions among the National Executive, the Board, and the broader CFES Team surrounding Federation matters.
\end{enumerate}
