\section{Internal Meetings}
\label{internal-meetings}

\subsection{Purpose}
\label{purpose}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  CFES holds four internal meetings a year for the CFES Team, with the aim of coordinating Federation initiatives, strategy, goals and the transition process.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Online Meetings}
\label{online-meetings}

\subsubsection{Attendance}
\label{attendance}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  All members of the Board members, Commissioners, and Activity Managers are expected to attend.
 \item
  Working group members are invited to attend.
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Summer Meeting}
\label{summer-meeting}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  Focus on group initiatives, with a review of action/strategic plan progress.
 \item
  Serves as the planning session for the Summit on Development of Engineering Societies.
 \item
  It is traditionally held in late June or July, and spans from a Friday to a Sunday.
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Fall Meeting}
\label{fall-meeting}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  Continued work on group initiatives, with further review of action strategic plan progress.
 \item
  Serves as the planning session for CELC.
 \item
  It is traditionally held in late October or early November, and spans from a Friday to a Sunday.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{In-Person/Hybrid Meetings}
\label{in-personhybrid-meetings}

\subsubsection{Attendance}
\label{attendance-1}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  Only members of the Board may attend with the following exceptions:

  \begin{enumerate}
   \item
    The Incoming and Outgoing Corporate Relations Commissioners may attend Spring Meeting.
   \item
    Activity Managers may attend Spring Meeting as per the Activity Agreement
  \end{enumerate}
 \item
  Exceptions can be made by the Board to allow Commissioners or Activity Managers to attend

  \begin{enumerate}
   \item
    The respective Vice President overseeing the Commissioner or Activity Manager must provide a letter to the Board detailing the tasks the individual will complete at the In-Person Meeting and why they must attend.
   \item
    These letters will be discussed and voted on at the Board meeting after the submission of the letter and require a majority vote to approve the individual's attendance.
  \end{enumerate}
 \item
  These meetings shall be accessible online for members of the Board who are unable to attend in-person and any Commissioner/Activity Manager who wishes to attend.
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Spring Meeting}
\label{spring-meeting}

\paragraph{Purpose}
\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  Spring Meeting primarily serves as a transition meeting between the outgoing and incoming Boards of the Federation.
 \item
  Time is given to facilitate role specific, subset specific, and group specific transition meetings.
 \item
  Initiatives from the prior year are transitioned to the new team, and the action planning process is begun.
 \item
  The strategic plan is reviewed and based on the progress made by the outgoing team, the incoming team sets objectives for their year.
 \item
  Spring Meeting also serves as an introduction of Engineers Canada to the Incoming team, with one day of Spring Meeting hosted by Engineers Canada at their office in Ottawa.
 \item
  The Engineers Canada day is planned by Engineers Canada and typically consists of:

  \begin{enumerate}
   \item
    An introduction to Engineers Canada;
   \item
    An overview of their priorities for the year;
   \item
    An update on the progress of the CFES from the Outgoing Board;
   \item
    An overview of the objectives of the Incoming Board; and
   \item
    Other sessions surrounding Engineers Canada operations such as the CEAB and Pathways to Engineering.
  \end{enumerate}

\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Planning}
\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  Planning for Spring Meeting is the responsibility of the Incoming and Outgoing Presidents and the Incoming and Outgoing VPFAs.
 \item
  Planning for Spring Meeting must commence no later than 1 month following CELC.
 \item
  Spring Meeting can be hosted no earlier than the third week of May to allow for the use of inexpensive university residences as the accommodations.
 \item
  Spring Meeting dates must be decided no later than the first week of March and must be based on the availability of both the incoming and outgoing teams, with the incoming team having a higher priority.
 \item
  Attendees must reach out to their faculty for travel funding no later than 1 month following CELC.

  \begin{enumerate}
   \item
    Failure to do so will result in them being ineligible for Travel Funding.
  \end{enumerate}

 \item
  Accommodations must be booked by the second week of March.
 \item
  The preliminary schedule must be sent to all attendees by April 1st and all Incoming and Outgoing Board members must be given the opportunity to provide feedback on the schedule.
 \item
  The final schedule must be sent to all attendees by May 1st.
 \item
  All attendees must apply for CFES Travel Funding if they require it by March 1st.
 \item
  Initial estimates during the planning process should be for 20 attendees.
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{CELC Pre-Week}
\label{celc-pre-week}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  Drive completion of group initiatives and action plan for progress against strategic plan.
 \item
  Familiarize oneself with the Activity's venue and meet the Activity OC.
 \item
  Finalize CELC member representative stream and begin planning for transitioning.
 \item
  This week is not in place for the Board, including the VP Services, to conduct responsibilities of the Activity Manager and the Activity OC outlined in their respective activity agreement.
 \item
  Pre-week is recommended to be held on the following timeline, respecting the competing needs of the holiday season and a busy CELC agenda:

  \begin{enumerate}
   \item
    If CELC begins on January 2, it is held from December 30 through January 2;
   \item
    If CELC begins on January 3, it is held from Dec 31 through January 3;
   \item
    And if CELC begins on January 4 or later, CELC Pre-Week is to begin 3 days before CELC itself.
  \end{enumerate}

 \item
  It is up to the Board, with consultation with the current CFES VPS and CELC Activity Manager, to reduce or increase the duration of pre-week based on the amount of outstanding items to be finalized.
 \item
  The CELC Activity Manager shall be informed of the final decision on the duration of pre-week no later than 1 week after the SDES held prior to the CELC conference.
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Expense Coverage}
\label{expense-coverage}

\paragraph{Best Practices}
\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  Individuals should act in good faith and be conscious of the use of member fees.
 \item
  Individuals should not seek to spend the maximum possible allowance given.
 \item
  Any purchase deemed excessive by the VPFA shall warrant a discussion between the individual, the President, the National Councillors, and the VPFA.
 \item
  This may also impact the individual's reimbursement.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Meal Allowance}
\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  The CFES shall cover up a set amount per meal for those attending internal meetings in-person.
 \item
  In order to receive the funding allowance for a given meal, the team member must be present at the meeting for that meal.
 \item
  The meal allowances are prices before tax and before tip.
 \item
  The CFES will reimburse individuals at the conclusion of the meeting based on their actual spending.
 \item
  Individuals who spend under the total allowance given to them will only be reimbursed for the amount they spent.
 \item
  Individuals who spend over the total allowance given to them will only be reimbursed the total allowance amount.
 \item
  Soft drinks, juices, water, and alcoholic drinks cannot be covered by the meal allowance.
 \item
  Members are expected to bring their own reusable water bottles to all internal meetings.
 \item
  Dessert cannot be covered by the meal allowance.
 \item
  If a meal is provided by a Partner, meal allowance will not be allocated for that meal.
 \item
  The meal allowance for each meal is defined below:

  \begin{enumerate}
   \item
    \$10 Breakfast
   \item
    \$10 Lunch
   \item
    \$15 Supper
  \end{enumerate}

\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Transportation During Internal Meetings}
\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  Transportation may only be covered if the distance between the accommodations and meeting venues is deemed to be not reasonable to walk by the VPFA and President.
 \item
  Case by case exceptions may be granted at the discretion of the VPFA and President.
 \item
  When transportation is deemed warranted, individuals may only be reimbursed for the most inexpensive, feasible transportation option which is at the discretion of the VPFA and President.
 \item
  Individuals who chose to use a more expensive transportation option will not be reimbursed for any of the cost.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Logistics}
\label{logistics}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  These meetings are organized by the National Executive.
 \item
  The CFES main budget shall be allocated to cover accommodations, food, and logistics of CFES Spring Meeting, Summer Meeting, and Fall Meeting.
 \item
  It shall also be allocated to cover food and logistics for CELC Pre-Week, while accommodations will be covered by the CELC Activity Manager's budget.
 \item
  In addition, the National Executive holds an Executive Transition Meeting for the National Executive and the individuals who have been elected to the National Executive for the following year.
 \item
  This meeting is organized by the National Executive currently in office and takes place after CELC, where elections are held, and before Spring Meeting.
\end{enumerate}
