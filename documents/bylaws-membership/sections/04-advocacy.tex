\section{Advocacy}
\label{advocacy}

\subsection{Document of Stances}
\label{advocacy-document-of-stances}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item The Document of Stances contains the official positions taken by the CFES.
 \item Stances contained in the Document may be added or modified by a ¾ Resolution of the General Meeting passed to that end.
 \item Stances may also be removed by a Resolution of the General Meeting.
 \item Modifications to the Document require two readings, and amendments to the modification may be put forth at the second reading.
  \begin{enumerate}
   \item Modifications to the Document may be made in exception to these requirements with approval of 90\% of Members present.
  \end{enumerate}
 \item The National Executive is responsible for alerting the Members to the procedure surrounding the Document of Stances in advance of SDES, as well as conducting background research to ensure that potential stances are generally beneficial for all Members, and alerting the Members to any associated implications in advance of the vote.
\end{enumerate}
