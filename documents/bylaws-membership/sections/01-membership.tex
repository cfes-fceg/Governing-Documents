\section{Membership}
\label{membership}

\subsection{Membership Fees}
\label{membership-membership-fees}

\subsubsection{Purpose}
\label{membership-fees-purpose}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item Paying the CFES membership fee maintains a Member’s status, which includes:
  \begin{enumerate}
   \item Eligibility for participation in CFES Activities and services
   \item Eligibility to bring forward motions at General Meetings
   \item Voting rights at General Meetings
  \end{enumerate}
 \item The membership fee for each Member shall be calculated based on its number of full-time engineering undergraduate students, plus any additional students who have opted into CFES membership.
 \item The membership fees shall be collected at the beginning of the CFES fiscal year, using headcounts from the prior academic year.
 \item Membership headcounts shall be submitted to the VPF by each member school no later than November 1st.
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Outstanding Membership Fees}
\label{membership-fees-outstanding-membership-fees}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item Members shall have no voting rights, until such time that membership fees are paid.
 \item A Member that did not attend any General Meeting and did not pay its membership fee on time must be contacted by the relevant Regional Ambassador to assess its future as a Member of the CFES.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Membership Withdrawal}
\label{membership-membership-withdrawal}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item Any Member may withdraw from the CFES by delivering a written resignation to the Vice-President Finance of the CFES.
 \item The withdrawal shall take effect the first day of the month immediately following reception of the notice.
 \item Any membership fees incurred to-date prior to withdrawal remain payable to the CFES.
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Grace Period}
\label{membership-withdrawal-grace-period}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item The withdrawn Member may immediately return to being a Member without needing to go through the formal membership application process, provided that by SDES of the fiscal year following their withdrawal the Member:
  \begin{enumerate}
   \item Notifies the Board with written proof of support from their engineering society
   \item Pays the membership fees to be up to date with dues
   \item Has not withdrawn twice within the last 12 months
  \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Member Student Opt-In}
\label{membership-member-student-opt-in}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item Members may decide to opt in all students they represent, including those who are not either enrolled in an accredited engineering program or enrolled in a program in the process of accreditation into the CFES membership.
 \item Members who wish to opt-in additional students to the CFES must submit this request to the Vice-President Finance before July 1st of the year prior to the membership fee due date.
 \item The CFES’ responsibility to Members opted in this way is limited to providing access to its Activities.
 \item Students who are not opted in shall not be permitted to attend CFES Activities or gain from the services it provides, unless an exception is granted by a Resolution of Board.
\end{enumerate}
