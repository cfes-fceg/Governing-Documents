\section{Other Meetings of the Members}
\label{other-meetings-of-the-members}

\subsection{Bootcamp}
\label{other-meetings-of-the-members-bootcamp}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item Bootcamp shall be an online gathering of Members and the CFES Team whose purpose is to host training sessions and discussions, along with networking.
 \item Bootcamp shall take place in June, or July.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Online Seasonal Update Meetings (OSUMs)}
\label{other-meetings-of-the-members-online-seasonal-update-meetings-osums}

\begin{enumerate}
 \item The CFES shall hold two remote meetings to bridge the gap between General Meetings.
 \item These meetings shall be an online gathering of Members and the CFES Team.
 \item The focus of these meetings shall be for the CFES Team to give updates to the Members on progress on their portfolios, projects and advocacy work.
 \item These meetings shall also include opportunities for Members to provide feedback.
 \item These meetings shall be scheduled with one taking place before SDES and another before CELC.
 \item Organization of these meetings and agendas are the responsibility of the Chair with support from relevant members of the CFES Team.
\end{enumerate}
